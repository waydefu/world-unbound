## Why

## Performance acceptance (new standard; also add it to `docs/process/visual-review.md` under a "Performance gate" heading)

The main-app GPU figure is **not** a frame budget: three.js `resolveTimestampsAsync` returns the *sum of per-pass begin→end durations* of the last frame (3 shadow passes + main + …); on tiled mobile GPUs passes overlap and include waits, and the GPU down-clocks when lightly loaded. Owner telemetry showed GPU p50 26.1 ms while frames held 60 FPS (p95 17.5 ms) — impossible for a real per-frame GPU time. So acceptance uses frame-interval-based evidence only:

On the owner's phone, med tier, each of cam 3 and cam 5: render scale stays 1.0 for 60 s at 60 FPS (no frame > 50 ms after warm-up), **and** the G1.0 load ramp on the spike page shows ≥ 30 % headroom. The orchestrator states the numbers in the PR. (10-minute soak at the end of G1.)

## Phase A — shared noise textures (`src/render/noiseTextures.ts`, new; pure generator in `src/env/` or `src/world/` if you want it unit-tested)

- At startup generate one **tileable** 256 × 256 RGBA8 texture on the CPU (deterministic, seeded): R,G = surface slope (∂h/∂x, ∂h/∂z) of a periodic multi-octave height field mapped to 0..255; B = the height itself; A = an independent periodic value noise (for breakup/grain). Use periodic gradient or value noise (lattice wraps at the tile size) so every channel tiles seamlessly. Generate mipmaps (`generateMipmaps = true`, `RepeatWrapping`, `LinearMipmapLinearFilter`, anisotropy 4 where supported).
- Unit test the pure generator: deterministic, values in range, seamless (left/right and top/bottom edges continue: compare finite differences across the seam with those inside).
- Account its bytes in `MemoryPartition.Gpu`.

## Phase B — ocean

1. Detail normals: replace all `mx_noise_float` calls with **two** samples of the noise texture's slope channels at world xz: scale `1/23 m` drifting along the wind, and `1/6.5 m` drifting at a different angle and speed; sum the decoded slopes (weights 1 and 0.6), scale by the weather slope factor and `smoothstep(400, 60, dist)`, and perturb the Gerstner normal: `N = normalize(Ng − vec3(sx, 0, sz))`. Lite tier: one sample at `1/23 m`.
2. Constant sky values move to the CPU: the zenith/hemisphere radiance used for body and foam lighting becomes a uniform set in `Environment.apply` from the sky summary (pre-exposure applied in `setExposure` like the other uniforms). The fragment shader keeps exactly **one** sky-table lookup (the reflection).
3. Glint: replace `pow(dot(R, sunDir), 3000)` by a normalised Gaussian lobe on the half vector, `exp(−(1 − dot(N, H)) / m²)` with `m² = 0.0004 + 0.002 · smoothstep(50, 800, dist) + slopeVariance`, scaled so the integrated energy stays ~constant (multiply by `1 / (π m²)` and clamp the result to ≤ 60000 after exposure). `slopeVariance` can be a constant per weather. Result: a glitter path that widens with distance instead of aliasing.
4. Subsurface (backlit crests): add `body += sssColour · sunLight · pow(max(dot(V, −sunDir_horizontal), 0), 4) · crest²` where `crest = clamp(vWaveY / Σamplitude, 0, 1)` and `sssColour = (0.02, 0.18, 0.15)`; fade it to 0 when the sun is below the horizon.
5. Foam breakup uses the noise texture's A channel (one more sample, shared with nothing) instead of noise.
6. Count after the change: ≤ 4 texture samples (2 noise + 1 sea floor + 1 sky; lite: 1 + 1 + 1) and **zero** `mx_noise_float` in the ocean shader. Grep must confirm.

## Phase C2 — deferred items from the G1.5 blind review (PR #20)

1. **Triplanar on steep terrain**: in `terrainMaterial.ts` (and `farTerrain.ts` if it samples noise), sample the noise texture with world-space triplanar blending where the slope is steep: weights from `abs(normal)^4` normalised; use the xz projection alone where `normal.y > 0.85` (saves samples on flat ground). This removes stretched streaks along the fall line.
2. **Shore foam and shallows** (ocean): the foam band must be thinner and broken, not a solid white ribbon — `foam = smoothstep(0.6, 0, depth) × breakup` with breakup from the noise texture A channel at `1/3 m`, and multiplied by `0.6`; shallow-water colour transitions over `depth 0 … 8 m` with `exp(−depth / 3)` and a sand-tinted shallow colour `(0.10, 0.12, 0.09)` at depth 0, so there is no hard turquoise edge.
3. **Night/rain exposure**: in `Environment.apply`, for overcast weather at night the exposure target makes the sky brighter than lit ground. Clamp the night key so a white surface lit only by sky ambient maps to ~0.08–0.12 of display white, and lift the ambient floor in rain by the overcast zenith (not only by `NIGHT_FLOOR`). Acceptance: cam 4 shows readable terrain silhouettes and the fjord as a distinct, slightly brighter band than the ground.
