## Ground rules

- Module layers (`scripts/audit.mjs`): pure state and maths in `src/env` (climate, wind) and `src/world` (vegetation rules, masks); GPU work in `src/render`; wiring in `src/app`. Files ≤ 350 lines (`src/app/App.ts` is at 339: add new wiring in new files, e.g. `src/app/vegetationBackend.ts`). `@hot` / `@amortized` tags; no allocation in `@hot` paths.

## Phase A — climate and wind (`src/env/climate.ts`, `src/env/wind.ts`, pure; `src/render/windTexture.ts`)

1. `ClimateState` v1: `{ season01, temperatureC, windDirRad, windSpeedMs, gust01 }` from `(seed, gameTimeSeconds, weather, dayOfYear)`. Season from the existing calendar (`src/env`). Wind base speed per weather (clear 3, cloudy 5, rain 8, storm 15 m/s; `// unverified`, tunable), direction from a slow seeded noise of time (changes over hours), gust01 from a faster seeded noise (seconds). Temperature: seasonal mean for 62° N coastal (`// unverified` fallback: Jan 0 °C, Jul 15 °C) plus a diurnal swing and an altitude lapse of −6.5 °C/km exposed as a function.
2. Fjord channelling (baked once from the DTM, `src/world/windChannel.ts`, pure): a 256² map (16 m cells) of the local "valley axis" direction (from smoothed terrain gradient / principal direction of low ground within ~300 m) and a shelter factor 0..1 (lee of ridges relative to the wind direction is computed at runtime from the axis and a sky-openness value baked here). Runtime wind at a point = rotate the global wind towards the valley axis by `channelling = (1 − openness)`, scale by shelter.
3. Wind texture (`src/render/windTexture.ts`): camera-centred 64 × 64 RGBA16F (or RGBA8 if half float is unavailable on WebGL2), 8 m cells (512 m square), texel-snapped toroidal scroll; RG = wind xz (m/s), B = gust phase, A = shelter. Updated on CPU at 10 Hz from Phase A.1–2; upload counted in the upload counter and GPU memory.
4. Ocean: replace the hard-coded `+z` drift in `src/render/oceanMaterial.ts` with the global wind direction/speed uniforms (detail-layer drift and wave choppiness scale gently with speed; keep the Gerstner set unchanged).
5. Snapshot: publish `ClimateState` in the environment snapshot / telemetry (`environment.windDirDeg`, `windSpeedMs`, `temperatureC`, `season01`) and one HUD token (`wind 6.2 m/s 245°`).
6. Tests: determinism (same inputs → same outputs), ranges, smooth over time (no jumps between consecutive seconds), channelling turns wind towards a synthetic valley axis.

## Phase B — vegetation mask (`src/world/vegetationMask.ts`, pure; built in the terrain worker with the other textures)

1. Inputs per lattice texel (same lattice and step as the material texture): height, 10 m-smoothed slope (as the CDLOD material bake), aspect, distance to sea water, a wetness proxy (flow accumulation or concavity from the smoothed height), and the existing material weights.
2. Output RGBA8 `vegetation` texture: R = meadow/grass density, G = heath/moss (above the treeline and on thin soil), B = tree/shrub density (CPU use later; may be 0 for now except as a colour tint), A = **bare** (rock / stone / sand: where nothing grows).
3. Rules (constants, documented):
   - Bare rock only where the 10 m slope exceeds `ROCK_SLOPE_DEG` (42°: the flora note found rockfall release at 40–45° in NVE guidance but no vegetation threshold; `// unverified`), or above the heath limit, or on steep gully walls; moderate slopes (≤ ~40°) stay vegetated → **this replaces the slope-only rock in the CDLOD material bake**: write the final rock weight into the material texture's G channel from this rule (keep `classifyTerrain` and its tests untouched; the CDLOD bake calls the new rule instead, the chunk path keeps the old one).
   - Treeline `TREELINE_M = 750` (between the coastal 500–600 m and inner-fjord 900–1100 m figures in the flora note; tunable, `// unverified for Hellesylt`); above it trees → 0, heath ↑; above `HEATH_LIMIT_M = TREELINE_M + 300` bare ↑.
   - Shore: sand/shingle band where height < 1.5 m next to the sea (existing sand rule), no grass on it.
   - **Man-made stone**: land texels with height 0–5 m that have sea water within 4 m on two roughly opposite sides (thin strips: breakwaters, piers), or a near-vertical drop to water (quay walls), get A = 1 (bare) and a separate flag in the material texture's B channel (reuse "earth" as `stone` for these texels, document it) so the terrain shader draws them as grey stone/concrete, not grass or sand.
   - Village: grass density reduced on the town disc (`TOWN_X/Z/RADIUS`) to short lawn values (tracks and buildings come later).
4. Tests: synthetic height fields — a 30° slope is vegetated, a 60° slope is bare, a 3 m-wide strip between two water bodies is flagged stone, a wide flat field is meadow; determinism.

## Phase C — grass rendering (`src/render/grass.ts`, `src/render/grassMaterial.ts`, `src/app/vegetationBackend.ts`)

Technique: camera-centred hashed cells (references: Duskward, Grassworks in `docs/reference/external-projects.md`); nothing stored per blade.

1. Cells: square cells of 8 m around the camera on a grid snapped to the cell size; rings: near blades to `NEAR_M` (low 25, med 40, high 60 m), mid cards to `MID_M` (low 60, med 100, high 150 m); beyond that the terrain shader carries the grass colour (and a subtle per-pixel "grass" normal from the noise texture). Frustum-cull cells on the CPU (allocation-free).
2. Blades: one instanced mesh of a 5–7-vertex tapered blade (2 triangles per segment, 3 segments near, 1 segment far). Per instance position derived **in the vertex shader** from `(cellId, instanceIndex)` with an integer hash (no per-instance buffers except one small per-cell uniform array/instance attribute with the cell origin). Density per tier (blades per m² near: low 8, med 16, high 28), multiplied by `vegetation.R` sampled at the blade's xz (`textureLoad` / point sample) — blades with density below their hash threshold collapse to zero-area (degenerate) so no branching on counts.
3. Height from the CDLOD height texture (same lattice, bilinear of 4 `textureLoad`s) so blades sit exactly on the terrain; normal from the normal texture for lighting.
4. Shape and species archetypes (from the flora note): `pasture` (short, 5–12 cm, fine), `hay meadow` (30–60 cm with seed heads; flower specks: yellow buttercup, white clover — a few % of blades get a tinted tip), `tussock` (Deschampsia-like, 40–80 cm, clumped: hash-based clumping by snapping a fraction of blades towards a per-cell clump centre). Choose archetype per blade from height above sea, wetness proxy and town disc (lawn = pasture).
5. Wind: blades sample the wind texture at their root; bend = base sway (wind speed) + gust wave travelling along the wind direction (phase from position · windDir and B channel) + per-blade flutter; bend increases with height along the blade (quadratic). Mid cards bend with the same function (whole card).
6. Colour and lighting: base colours match the terrain grass colours at the blade root (no visible seam where blades fade into the terrain); tip lighter; translucency (backlit sun: `pow(saturate(dot(V, −L)), 4)`); season tint from `season01` (spring fresh green, summer yellow-green, autumn straw); AO towards the root. Receive shadows from the CSM on med/high (blades do not cast).
7. Fade: blade height and density fade smoothly to 0 at the ring edge (dither or shrink, no pop); cards fade in over the near→mid boundary.
8. Tier switches: low = cards only out to 60 m (no blades) unless profiling allows; `?grass=0` disables grass for A/B.
9. Telemetry: `world.grass { cells, blades, cards, ms? }`, triangles per pass include grass; HUD token `grass 38k`.

## Phase D — water and shore fixes

1. Enclosed basins: bake (in the worker, from the sea mask) a "fetch/openness" value per coast-map texel: the fraction of 16 directions in which open water extends > 150 m. Inside enclosed basins (openness < 0.25) the shore-foam band and crest foam fade to 0 and the shallow-colour band narrows (water stays calm). Store it in a free channel of an existing coast texture if one exists, else a new R8 texture; account bytes.
2. Shallow band: narrow the turquoise band to depth 0–4 m (was 0–8) where the coast slope is steep (quays, breakwaters).
3. Cam 5: the breakwater/pier in the lower middle must read as a grey stone strip with water on both sides and no foam outline inside the basin.
